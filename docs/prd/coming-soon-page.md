# PRD: Coming Soon Holding Page

## Problem Statement

Trusted Assistance needs a professional web presence while the full site is under development. Without one, visitors to the domain see nothing — a missed opportunity to build brand awareness and signal credibility.

## Target Users

- Potential clients discovering Trusted Assistance
- Partners and collaborators checking the brand online
- Anyone arriving at the domain via referral or search

## Solution

A single-page static "Coming Soon" site built with **Astro**, featuring bold and vibrant branding that conveys energy and trustworthiness.

## Page Content

- Brand name: **Trusted Assistance**
- Tagline (suggested): *"Expert guidance you can count on"*
- "Coming Soon" message with a brief teaser sentence
- Contact email or social link for enquiries

## Visual Direction

- **Bold & vibrant** — strong accent colors, high contrast, energetic feel
- Modern typography, clean layout
- Responsive (mobile-first)

## Success Criteria

1. Page loads in under 1 second on a standard connection (static HTML)
2. Fully responsive across mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports
3. Passes Lighthouse accessibility score of 90+
4. Displays brand name, tagline, coming soon message, and contact info
5. Deploys as a static site (zero runtime dependencies)

## Out of Scope

- Email signup / newsletter form
- Countdown timer
- CMS or admin panel
- Multi-page navigation
- Backend services
